## Distinguishing visual tells (Quarters vs. Palms) — synthesized from narration + frames
- **Quarters (base):** both corners play "2 read" independently — soft/off alignment, eyes on #2, will squat on a #2 out/curl but carry #2 vertical. Plays out symmetrically; nothing changes coverage shape regardless of what happens on the backside.
- **Palms (2-read variant of Cover 4):** only converts to true "palms" reaction **if #2 releases to the flat** — at that moment the corner reacts down onto the flat/curl and the safety (not the corner) takes over the vertical threat from #1. If #2 does NOT go flat, "it stays quarters" — same shell, no different look pre-snap.
- Because the conversion is a **post-snap read trigger, not a different pre-snap shell**, the video's frames show no visible pre-snap tell distinguishing Quarters from Palms — the difference only becomes visible after the snap once #2's release is known, matching Sam's repeated point that "it's going to play out as quarters" unless #2 goes to the flat.
- Palms requires **fast, confident corners** per his commentary — the frames back this: any hesitation/squat-too-long by the corner (as at 08:34) leaves the vertical threat uncovered because the safety help is now occupied elsewhere (helping over #3 or the deep half).
