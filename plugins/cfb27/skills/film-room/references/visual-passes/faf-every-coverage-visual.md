# Fourth and Film — Every Coverage Explained — Visual Pass

Diagram style throughout: static all-22-style whiteboard shot (green field, white
position-label boxes: SS/FS/CB/LB/DE/DT/TN visible at their alignment spots) with
hand-drawn **yellow circles = zone drop areas** and **yellow arrows = defender
movement/rotation**. Same base formation reused for every coverage so the drawn
elements are the only thing that changes — useful as a template for comparing our
charted `coverage_shell` field against a canonical diagram.

## Coverage-by-coverage: what's drawn

- **Cover 0** [00:03–01:15]: No yellow circles at all in the deep field — only a red
  X marking the blitz gap and yellow arrows tracking blitzers into the backfield.
  Diagnostic tell: **empty deep field, arrows converging on the QB spot.** Frame:
  `ROliJ27Br9A-0005-cover0-blitz-x-formation.jpg`.
- **Cover 1** [01:15–02:31]: One large yellow circle centered deep (FS) with press
  arrows on the outside CBs; no second deep circle. Diagnostic tell: **exactly one
  deep circle, dead center.** Frame: `ROliJ27Br9A-0120-cover1-single-high-safety.jpg`.
- **Cover 1 Robber** [02:31–03:44]: Same single deep FS circle as Cover 1, plus a
  second, smaller circle sitting ~5-10yd underneath in the middle (the robber) that
  is NOT tied to a receiver — floats independent of the routes. Diagnostic tell:
  **a hovering mid-level circle with no man-coverage line attached to it.** Frame:
  `ROliJ27Br9A-0235-cover1-robber-lurk-zone.jpg`.
- **Cover 2 Zone** [03:44–05:04]: Two large deep circles side by side (~15yd depth),
  covering left/right halves; underneath zone circles for LBs/CBs are smaller and
  sit shallow. Diagnostic tell: **two symmetric deep half-circles with a visible
  gap between them (the deep-middle hole).** Frame:
  `ROliJ27Br9A-0348-cover2-zone-two-deep-halves.jpg`.
- **Cover 2 Man** [05:04–06:22]: Same two deep circles as Cover 2 Zone, but now the
  CBs have tight press arrows/lines drawn directly to receivers instead of zone
  circles underneath. Diagnostic tell: **two deep circles + man-to-man lines
  underneath (mixed zone/man drawing).** Frame:
  `ROliJ27Br9A-0514-cover2-man-press-corners.jpg`.
- **Cover 2 Buzz** [06:22–07:26]: Not separately captured as a distinct diagram
  frame in this pass (transitions too fast in the still-frame format); described
  verbally as starting like Cover 3 pre-snap, safety "buzzes" down, CB bails deep.
  Re-check [06:45–06:57] if a dedicated frame is needed later.
- **Tampa 2** [07:26–08:44]: Two deep safety circles plus a THIRD circle in the
  deep-middle explicitly numbered/labeled near the MLB position with a drop arrow
  straight down the middle — forming the signature three-defender deep triangle.
  Diagnostic tell: **middle circle sits noticeably deeper than the LB line,
  vertically between the two safety circles.** Frame:
  `ROliJ27Br9A-0730-tampa2-mlb-drop-triangle.jpg`.
- **Cover 3** [08:44–09:57]: Three large, evenly-spaced deep circles spanning the
  full width of the field (both CBs + FS), described in narration as a "picket
  fence." Diagnostic tell: **three equal-size deep circles, evenly spaced,
  symmetric.** Frame: `ROliJ27Br9A-0853-cover3-three-deep-picket-fence.jpg`.
- **Cover 3 Cloud** [09:57–11:07]: Same three-deep picture as Cover 3, but one
  outside circle is drawn smaller/shallower (the "cloud" CB staying at the flat)
  with a rotation arrow showing the safety sliding over to replace his deep third.
  Diagnostic tell: **asymmetric — one side's deep circle is fed by a rotation
  arrow from the safety position, not the CB.** Frame:
  `ROliJ27Br9A-1001-cover3-cloud-corner-rotate.jpg`.
- **Cover 3 Buzz** [11:07–12:36]: Shown as two deep circles pre-snap (Cover 2 look)
  with one circle sprouting a sharp downward arrow into an underneath zone at the
  snap, while the corners' circles appear deep — net three-deep result. Diagnostic
  tell: **arrow originates from a pre-snap deep circle and terminates underneath**
  (opposite direction from Cover 3 Cloud's arrow).
- **Cover 4 / Quarters** [12:36–13:47]: Four large, evenly-spaced deep circles
  spanning the field — the widest, most symmetric deep shell of the video.
  Diagnostic tell: **four equal circles, evenly spaced, "wall" look.** Frame:
  `ROliJ27Br9A-1241-cover4-quarters-four-deep-even.jpg`.
- **Cover 6** [13:47–15:07]: Field split down the middle — one side has a single
  large deep circle (Cover 2 half), the other side has two smaller circles closer
  together (Cover 4 quarter pair). Diagnostic tell: **visibly asymmetric deep
  shell, 1-circle side vs. 2-circle side.** Frame:
  `ROliJ27Br9A-1355-cover6-asymmetric-2over4.jpg`.

## Notes for charting agents

- The same base 11-personnel alignment (labels SS/FS, LB/LB/LB, CB/DE/DT/DT/DE/CB)
  is reused across every coverage diagram in this video — only the yellow
  circles/arrows differ. Good as a clean baseline to overlay against CFB27 game
  footage pre-snap alignment.
- Circle **count and spacing in the deep field** is the single most reliable visual
  discriminator: 0 deep circles = Cover 0, 1 = Cover 1/Robber, 2 = Cover 2 family,
  3 = Cover 3 family/Tampa 2, 4 = Cover 4. Cover 6 is the outlier (asymmetric 1-vs-2).
  Arrows layered on top disambiguate the sub-variants (buzz/cloud/robber/man vs zone).
