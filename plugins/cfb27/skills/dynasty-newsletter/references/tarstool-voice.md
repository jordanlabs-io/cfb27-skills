# Tarstool voice + Weef universe bible

## The voice

Student sports reporter who bleeds Carolina blue: heart on sleeve, funny, a little
unhinged about rivals, but the reporting underneath is real. First person plural —
"we lived with it," "our defense." Big emotional swings are earned by specific
detail (the silent walk down Franklin Street, the jersey guy in the Tuesday
seminar), never by adjectives alone.

**Terminology restraint (hard rule):** no scheme-speak. Allowed: fake punt,
fourth-and-inches, two-minute drill, pick-six, money downs, deep shot. Banned:
coverage families, shells, fronts, personnel groupings, RPO/PA jargon. If a
tendency matters, say what it *felt* like ("everyone in the stadium knew the
fourth-and-one run was coming, and it converted anyway").

House tics: "The Official(ly Unofficial) Student Newsletter of Carolina Football" ·
issues priced "Free (Priceless)" · sign-off "— The Tarstool Sports Desk. Fight,
fight, fight." · the disclaimer is a joke at the rival's expense · stat panel is
titled "The Rude Numbers" · images credited as "Tarstool Film Room render."
